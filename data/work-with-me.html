Work with me

I’m a writer, outdoor instructor, Expeditions Manager at British Exploring Society and trainee Advanced Wilderness Therapeutic Guide. I help people find their way through words and through wild places. Yes, that’s too many hats. Here’s what they’re for.

Nature connection and wilderness therapy

Small-group journeys outdoors, designed around what the people on them need: time, space, a bit of challenge and the company of the natural world. In 2025 I launched Night Shift, a therapeutic journey through darkness for men, with a dusk-till-dawn hike in the New Forest.

If you run a group, a school, a charity or a team that could do with getting outside together, let’s talk about what that could look like.

Talks and storytelling

Stories from twenty years of slow adventures: cycling to the Sahara, riding towards Syria along the routes of migration, and eight years with Thighs of Steel, the bike ride that raised over £760,000 for refugee-led projects. For schools, clubs, festivals and anyone who likes a good story about getting lost on purpose.

Writing and editing

I’ve worked as a writer and editor for over fifteen years. I can help turn raw ideas into a clear outline, give a hefty structural edit to your draft, or do a precise line edit before it goes to print. I know adventure, travel, migration, nature, activism, popular science and comedy best. I helped Alastair Humphreys structure The Doorstep Mile. More about writing and editing.

Comedy writing

I’m one half of the team behind BBC Radio sitcom Foiled, with Beth Granville: a five-star Edinburgh Fringe show and three radio series with guests including Miles Jupp, Ralf Little and Sir Derek Jacobi. Scripts, punch-ups and workshops.
