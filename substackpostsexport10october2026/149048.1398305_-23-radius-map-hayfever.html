ON THE ROAD

After joining the Thighs of Steel crew on Monday for a Bank Holiday cycle to the Quadrangle, I vow two things:

  1. Be more waterproof.

  2. Do more "short" cycles of 40-50km.

Hence the shoot-to-kill map above. That inner green ring is 30km as the crow flies, which usually levels out at ~45km as the bike rides.

Theoretically, I can pick anywhere in that ring and guarantee adventure - doughnuts, rainstorms and log fires. Blowing the smoke out of my lungs before catching a train back to the city.

I want to make these microadventures [a la Humphreys] a bigger part of my life. Ironic that I've spent 10 minutes inside a computer making a fancy map to remind me to get out more.

IN THE BODY

Hayfever getting to you as bad as it is to me?

Sustained use of certain anti-histamines can increase your risk of developing dementia in later life [Original Paper | Write Up].

For long term daily use, try loratadine (i.e. Clarityn, but Boots do a generic version) instead of Cetirizine hydrochloride (Piriteze).

Bless you.
