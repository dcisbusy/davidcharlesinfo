What is UKHIP?

Putting Humanity back into Humanitarian Intervention


UKHIP is the United Kingdom Humanitarian Intervention Party and is more of an idea than a political party: an idea that every human, no matter where they come from, deserves to be treated equally and with respect.

This is manifestly not happening in Calais, where people from some of the most dangerous places on earth - Syria, Afghanistan, Eritrea, Sudan - are being forced to live the lives of animals, scraping a living from the waste of our society, beating their heads against the walls of our border.

The news from Calais isn't great. Calais Migrant Solidarity have a catalogue of attacks on migrants and activists by fascists. The 'Jungles' and squats where the migrants are living have all been served eviction notices, which will probably happen at the beginning of April.

(If you're wondering why I'm supporting UKHIP and the migrants in Calais: it's only luck. It's only luck that I was born with a British passport and can happily go trampling all over the world, while billions of other humans cannot. Not only that; we punish them for trying.)
