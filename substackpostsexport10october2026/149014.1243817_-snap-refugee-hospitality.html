Refugee Hospitality

Greetings from Baden-Wurttemberg, Germany!

We've just spent a delicious 24 hours with a group of refugees from Syria and Lebanon, after one of them took pity on us boiling eggs in the town square.

Their openness and hospitality was a delight to experience. It really felt like they were throwing everything they could at life, determined to make the best of whatever they had - even if that was only a 'plastic' room and good friends.
