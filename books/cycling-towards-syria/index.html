Cycling towards Syria

London to Vienna by bike in 2016, following the routes of migration

In May 2016 David Charles set off from London to cycle 3,000 miles along the routes people were taking into Europe, collecting stories from refugees, volunteers and officials on the way: Calais and Grande-Synthe, the Belgian asylum system, Germany’s welcome and its limits, and a refugee camp in Vienna.

16 chapters, about 10,000 words. Free to read and share.
