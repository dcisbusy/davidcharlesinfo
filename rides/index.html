Rides

Long bike rides, drawn from my notes. Solid lines follow my route between the places I slept each night; dashed lines are ferries. A question mark is a place still to check.

Lines follow roads and paths between the places in my notes, so they show roughly the way I went, not every turn.

Round Britain by bike, 2011

58 days · 4,110 miles · 23 nights in a bivvy bag · The book

  1. Day 1 Near Hawkwell17 July 2011 · 58.3 miles · BivvyTrain from Leyton to Epping for the start.There's no wave this time and I simply roll down the hill alone and turn left at the end of Centre Drive.Then I notice: my bags are indeed still on the rack, but the rack is no longer attached to the bike.
  2. Day 2 Kirton, Suffolk18 July 2011 · 82 miles · BivvyHunger squeezes in on me as I slide down into Maldon, famous for the production of Jamie Oliver's favourite sea salt.We chug out of the harbour and into the estuary, the North Sea rushing beyond.
  3. Day 3 Near Corton19 July 2011 · 80 miles · BivvyThis doesn't feel so much like an attempt to cycle around Britain, more like a race to see which will break first: my bike, my body or my brain.
  4. Day 4 Near Sheringham20 July 2011 · 78.09 miles · BivvyMy visit to the homely Hemsby Post Office runs on the following principle: if it doesn't help me sleep or cycle, it's going.
  5. Day 5 King's Lynn21 July 2011 · 65 miles · With friendsMy bike is now reduced to nothing more than a pack animal - and a rather recalcitrant one at that, back wheel scraping and sawing.Malcolm introduces himself, spins his bike around and we push together through the park.
  6. Day 6 London22 July 2011 · rest day · With friendsRest day: train from King's Lynn to London and back.It comes down to this: either I buy San Anselmo or I give up altogether.
  7. Day 7 Near Kirton, Lincolnshire23 July 2011 · 52.82 miles · BivvyAt last I jump into the saddle of my San Anselmo.It's a hay field, dotted with straw bales strung up and covered in white plastic, like enormous sugar lumps, offering ideal cover from the road.
  8. Day 8 Near Beverley24 July 2011 · 110.73 miles · BivvyBut halfway across the Humber suspension bridge I do allow myself a pause for a celebratory garage sandwich and a glug of water.I lean on the parapet and look out to the estuary, flat water running sky blue out to sea.
  9. Day 9 Near Ravenscar25 July 2011 · 87.91 miles · BivvyAfter the wolds and wealds of the last two days, I finally hit the coast again at Flamborough Head.The sea is a dimpled face, wine-black, accented white expressions.
  10. Day 10 Middlesbrough26 July 2011 · 59.05 miles · With friendsTrain to Newcastle for the night with a friend, and back the next morning.Whitby sort of tumbles down from the headland, split in two by the River Esk.My plate arrives and, seizing my fork, I skewer the happy proof that I've finally reached The North: black pudding.
  11. Day 11 Newcastle27 July 2011 · 66.05 miles · With friendsRode without the heavy bags, which waited in Newcastle.I feel invincible, like Hercules on a bike, bursting up hills that would have defeated me a couple of days ago.I cross the Tyne, the famous steel fretwork of the Tyne Bridge framing triangles of blue.
  12. Day 12 Near Seahouses28 July 2011 · 69 miles · BivvyA couple of hours later, I'm cycling past a panoramic view of Dunstanburgh Castle ruins, rising like broken teeth from the rocky headland on the edge of the world.
  13. Day 13 Edinburgh29 July 2011 · 104.25 miles · BivvyAhead is a sign that reads, Welcome to Scotland; behind me a sign says, Welcome to England.
  14. Day 14 Tentsmuir Forest, near Leuchars30 July 2011 · 75.88 miles · BivvyCamped just inside the forest near Leuchars.From the more modern (read prosaic) road suspension bridge, the Forth Bridge defies my attempts to capture its majesty on camera.It shimmers across the Firth of Forth like a mirage, its three pyramidical cantilevers rising into the white sky, not so unlike Giza.
  15. Day 15 Perth31 July 2011 · 48.71 miles · With friendsLost in Tentsmuir Forest for an hour before finding the way out.The next morning, I try to find my way through the forest, but I'm trapped in Hansel and Gretal's nightmares.Those last ten miles around and around Tentsmuir have taken me over the thousand mile mark.
  16. Day 16 Lunan1 August 2011 · 61.49 miles · BivvyI finally throw down my bicycle near Lunan, on the slope of a hill, under a prickly pine.It's dry and the stars are laid out before me: a whole universe of them.
  17. Day 17 Methlick2 August 2011 · 89.3 miles · Hotel YfanviewYthan View, named for the river on which Methlick stands, is an old inn, a few rooms above a whitewashed pub, basket flowers hanging from the eaves.
  18. Day 18 Elgin3 August 2011 · 76.11 miles · With friendsThe cow, it transpires, is a local celebrity: the Turra Coo.At Cullen, golf courses jostle with the coast, caddies cluster on the greens, the beaches below brace themselves against the rolling sea, windbreakers planted in the sand.
  19. Day 19 Inverness4 August 2011 · 51.39 miles · Youth hostelIt is, I suppose, appropriate to arrive in Macbeth's city in thunder, lightning or in rain.
  20. Day 20 Past Helmsdale ?5 August 2011 · 98.58 miles · BivvyThe four-car ferry arrives and all us cyclists roll on, leaving two late-coming cars to wait for the next.He sits before me like a modern Hephaestus, whose crafting skill will make my Odyssey or end it.
  21. Day 21 Kirkwall6 August 2011 · 83.07 miles · Overnight ferryFor those of you who haven't met a midge, they're just like fairies, except they want to kill you.I drop my bike to the ground, stagger across the sands at Duncansby Head and dabble my burning feet in the mineral shallows.
  22. Day 22 Lerwick7 August 2011 · 1.5 miles · Youth hostelOvernight ferry from Kirkwall to Lerwick.The ferry arrives in Lerwick in the early hours.Rain steeps the streets, but players in short sleeve woolly jumpers kneel and bowl on the green lawn opposite.
  23. Day 23 Kirkwall8 August 2011 · 18.62 miles · Youth hostelA short ride around Shetland, then the ferry back to Orkney.I now understand why Shetland ponies are built so stocky, so low to the ground: aerodynamics.I buy eighteen Twixes for the crossing back to Orkney.
  24. Day 24 Thurso9 August 2011 · 87.62 miles · Youth hostelSkara Brae has a lot to live up to after the childish delights of photographing myself at the sign for Twatt.A barrel-shaped chapel squats on the side of the road, built by Italian prisoners during the Second World War.
  25. Day 25 Kinlochbervie10 August 2011 · 94.94 miles · B&BBut the beaches are white crescent moons shining between the black gneiss cliffs and the black sea; as good as any in the world.
  26. Day 26 Stornoway11 August 2011 · 65.54 miles · Youth hostelElspeth is running the toaster, Morag is pushing pills.I don't know if you're aware, but the west coast of Scotland is fractal.
  27. Day 27 Lochmaddy12 August 2011 · 71.72 miles · Youth hostelI have to pedal as hard as I can downhill into the wind, which guzzles around my head like the crashing waves of the sea.
  28. Day 28 Mallaig13 August 2011 · 62.36 miles · Youth hostelThe weather isn't up to much, but, after all that fuss on Uist, I wouldn't want it any other way: these mountains look good in grey.I race myself to the ferry at Armadale and arrive with enough time to crack open an ice cream.
  29. Day 29 Oban14 August 2011 · 83.48 miles · Youth hostelI weave southerly between the lochs: Morar, nan Uamh, Ailort, Moidart, Shiel and Sunart.But the sea is smiling, speckled black, gulls dipping for fish, and we chug on through peace.
  30. Day 30 Irvine15 August 2011 · 102.01 miles · B&BI look down on the harbour: the ferry doors are just closing.Arran is Scotland in miniature, divided between highland and lowland, with Corbett peaks and Marilyn hills.
  31. Day 31 Glengap Forest, near Gatehouse of Fleet16 August 2011 · 85.66 miles · BivvySo, after nine straight nights with an easy roof over my head, tonight I return to the bivvy, finding a dark spot in Glengap Forest, in the belly of the promised Galloway.
  32. Day 32 Easton marshes17 August 2011 · 106.69 miles · BivvyGalloway is the gentle end of Scotland and I cross the border at Gretna, where the sun comes out to greet me.The sun sets picturesque over Easton marshes and I discover a cow pat among my bedding.
  33. Day 33 Broughton-in-Furness18 August 2011 · 83.56 miles · BivvyBesides, who could resist climbing a mountain called Biker Fell?Only when I blink three times do I see that it's actually called Birker Fell.
  34. Day 34 Near Inglewhite19 August 2011 · 89.12 miles · BivvySomewhere near Levens, I'm innocently cycling along when I get mauled by a butterfly, flapping around my face and glasses, clutching at me.
  35. Day 35 Liverpool20 August 2011 · 48.86 miles · International InnThe sunlight glitters in the eye of the Liver birds.
  36. Day 36 Glanwydden21 August 2011 · 79.57 miles · BivvyI turn the corner of the country and notice that I must be in Wales: vowels have disappeared from the road signs.
  37. Day 37 Bangor22 August 2011 · 27.44 miles · Adelphi (hotel)Train from Bangor to Liverpool to meet Mum and Dad: a night at the Adelphi.In the morning, I take the last twenty-five miles to the nearest town, to fulfil a lifelong dream of eating bangers in Bangor.
  38. Day 38 Near Llandecwyn station23 August 2011 · 45.39 miles · BivvyTrain back to Bangor, then on round the mainland, skipping Anglesey.With the soft sands for a mattress, I fall asleep on the beach, nibbling the last crumbs of ginger cake for dinner.
  39. Day 39 Lampeter24 August 2011 · 104 miles · Youth hostelNot Knowing, I'm not worried about where I am, where I'm going or how fast I'm going wherever I'm going.
  40. Day 40 Whitesands25 August 2011 · 78.78 miles · Youth hostelAt last I reach St David's, my namesake at the end of the road, the western tip of Wales.
  41. Day 41 Llangain26 August 2011 · 79 miles · BivvyThat cycle computer has been with me since London.As I cycle through the delightfully named Stepaside, steady exercise cleanses my mind and shows me how lucky I've been.
  42. Day 42 Cardiff27 August 2011 · 104.14 miles · With friendsAs I tuck in, I confess to myself that Wales has been difficult: six hundred miles of wet weather and humpback hills.I sprint on down the Taff Trail, into Cardiff.
  43. Day 43 Dani's barge on the canal near Bath ?28 August 2011 · 80 miles · With friendsI celebrate England by joining the queue for a pizza buffet in Bristol.I reach the canal at Bath and follow a line of puddles like cat's eyes on the tow path, wondering when I'm going to fall into the drink.
  44. Day 44 Dani's barge on the canal near Bath ?29 August 2011 · rest day · With friendsRest day on the barge, with a swim by the Dundas aqueduct.We wander down to the Dundas aqueduct and jump into the gasping river Avon.After breakfast, we go into Bath, for another breakfast.
  45. Day 45 5m from Taunton ?30 August 2011 · 68.63 miles · BivvyWhat is perhaps more surprising is that (aside from the exploding tyre incident) this is my first puncture, after more than three thousand miles.Leaving the bike behind, I wind myself around and around the terraces of the Tor, like the winding of a spinning top filling with kinetic energy, as I rise to the summit.
  46. Day 46 Yarde Orchard31 August 2011 · 95.37 miles · Youth hostelI make a deliberate detour when I see a sign pointing to a hamlet called Charles.The detour to my eponymous village happens to be up a one in three hill.
  47. Day 47 Newquay1 September 2011 · 93.72 miles · BivvySo I wheel my bike under the goalposts, to the shelter of a couple of trees and a patch of nettles.I fall asleep dreaming of Land's End and turning a third corner, ready for the home straight.
  48. Day 48 Porkellis2 September 2011 · 90.61 miles · BivvyAll the way round the coast via St Ives and Land's End, but not round the Lizard.By the time I reach St Ives, Cornwall's clarity of light is turning everything into a watercolour painting.I stop for the night in a field near Porkellis, slightly concerned that I'm sleeping in a tractor's parking space.
  49. Day 49 Plymouth3 September 2011 · 84.98 miles · Youth hostelBut we're in and I perch in the prow, one arm around San Anselmo, like an old friend.I zip through the woods to Fowey, a riverine town I fall in love-at-first-sight with.
  50. Day 50 Exeter4 September 2011 · 70.38 miles · Youth hostelTo the east, Torquay squats under ranked clouds; to the west, Brixham catches the last rays of sunshine through a pot hole in the grey cover.
  51. Day 51 Beer5 September 2011 · 42.55 miles · B&BWe scoot through Otterton, Sidmouth, Salcombe Regis, hiding between hedgerows and cranking up the Jurassic cliffs, 185 million years of geology under our tyres.
  52. Day 52 Weymouth6 September 2011 · 42 miles · B&BIt looks like a fantasia cake, topped with pine grove candles, crumbs falling into the sea, served on a plate of sand.
  53. Day 53 Portsmouth7 September 2011 · 94 miles · With friendsNight at a friend's in Winchester, by train from Portsmouth and back.I race down the long straight Ferry Road from Studland down to the Sandbanks chain ferry, which cuts off the neck of Poole harbour and saves me a twenty-five mile road trip.From a plastering of posters, I discover that Bestival, the island's music festival, starts tomorrow.
  54. Day 54 Telscombe8 September 2011 · 75.94 miles · Youth hostelSo I throw my bike over the fence, where it lands with a jarring crash on the other side.
  55. Day 55 Dover9 September 2011 · 103.2 miles · BivvyThat means today is my last chance to do two things that I loved all the way around Britain: cycling a hundred miles and sleeping rough.In a heart-warming tribute to my first night's bivvy, I have slugs as bed fellows.
  56. Day 56 Whitstable10 September 2011 · 53.69 miles · With familyMile 4,000 is archetypal, exactly how I imagined round Britain.I am the coast: the path drops straight off into the sea on my right.
  57. Day 57 Sanford Walk housing co-op, New Cross11 September 2011 · 73.89 miles · HomeArriving back in London, after all those miles, is like arriving in a foreign city that I saw in a dream once; it surprises me with its familiarity.
  58. Day 58 5 Centre Drive, Epping12 September 2011 · 23.4 miles · HomeI bump up onto the pavement and let San Anselmo come to a silent stop.I climb off and lean the bike against a gas meter.Fifty-eight days to cycle around the entire coastline of Britain, to heal from the loss of love and the loved.
