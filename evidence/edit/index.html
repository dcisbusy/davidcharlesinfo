How to lay it out
  • ## Heading starts a section. Text straight after it is the section’s introduction.
  • ### Study name — ★★★ starts a card. The stars set its strength rating (★ to ★★★★); leave them off for a card with no rating.
  • In a card, **Use:** and **Caveat:** start the “Use” (with its Copy button) and “Caveat” boxes.
  • Write citations as Surname et al. (2019) or (Surname et al., 2019): they link to the matching entry under ## References.
  • A reference is one line: - Surname, A. et al. (2019). Title. *Journal*. [doi](https://doi.org/…)
  • **bold**, *italic*, [text](https://…) and - lists work as usual.
  • A source cited some other way (a report, a project) needs a link added by hand in scripts/make_evidence.py (PHRASE_LINKS), or just write the link in: [Leeds research](https://…).
