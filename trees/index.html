Before you use this map

This map is for education and interest only. It is not a field guide and must not be used to identify any plant, or to decide whether anything is safe to eat, touch or use as medicine.

Do not pick, eat or use any part of any tree because of this map. The councils' records can be out of date or wrong, a tree may have been replaced, and many edible plants have poisonous look-alikes. The notes describe a species in general, not the tree in front of you, and they may be incomplete or mistaken.

Medicinal notes describe traditional and pharmaceutical uses. They are not medical advice: never treat yourself or anyone else with a plant, and talk to a doctor or pharmacist first.

Street and park trees can be polluted, sprayed or fouled by dogs, and they belong to the council or landowner. Keep children and pets away from poisonous trees.

If you think someone has eaten something poisonous, call NHS 111, or 999 in an emergency, and take a sample of the plant with you. For pets, call your vet.

You use this map and its information entirely at your own risk. David Charles accepts no responsibility for any harm, illness or loss from using it.

Tree map

Every tree the councils have mapped around Crystal Palace, Upper and South Norwood, Penge, Beckenham, Sydenham, Forest Hill and Dulwich: street trees, park trees and trees on estates. Tap a dot to find out what it is. Allow location and the map follows you on your walk.

Tick Edible, Medicinal or Poisonous to see only those trees, and tap one for what it's used for and what it can do to you. These are general notes about the species, not about the tree in front of you: never eat anything you can't identify for certain, ask a doctor before using any plant as medicine, and think twice about street trees beside busy roads or at dog height. Read the full disclaimer. The notes and their sources are in a spreadsheet.

Some places have far fewer trees than you'd expect: the councils only record trees they look after, and some (Croydon especially) have mapped very few.
