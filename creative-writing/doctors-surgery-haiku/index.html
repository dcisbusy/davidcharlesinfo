Doctor’s Surgery Haiku

8 July 2009 · Creative Writing

A letter stolen,
Taken from a postman’s bag;
Bad news won’t arrive.

***

Dampy skin otters,
Swimming against the current;
Do they realise?

***

Men not quite aged,
Clinging to the cliff of life;
Why don’t they let go?

***
